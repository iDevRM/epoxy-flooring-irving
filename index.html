What Is Epoxy Flooring in Irving?

Epoxy flooring in Irving is a resin coating that bonds to bare concrete and turns a dusty, stained slab into a sealed, easy-to-clean surface. Most of our work is home garages, but the same systems go down in shops, warehouses, patios and restaurant kitchens.

A real coating is not paint. It's a multi-coat system: a penetrating base coat, decorative vinyl flakes or metallic pigment, and a clear topcoat that takes the wear. Applied over ground concrete, it resists oil, brake fluid, pool chemicals and the grit that tracks in on tires.

Homeowners usually call us when the garage floor starts to look rough, when they're getting a house ready to sell, or when a cheap DIY kit has started peeling.

When Do You Need Epoxy Flooring?

You need epoxy flooring when bare concrete stops holding up to how you use the space. These are the situations that bring most Irving callers to us.

Dusting and stains

The slab sheds powder, holds oil drips and never looks clean no matter how often you hose it out.

Peeling DIY coating

A store-bought kit is lifting under the tires or flaking at the door line. It has to be ground off before a real system goes down.

New cracks

Hairline or settled cracks have opened up as the clay under the slab dried out and swelled back. They're worth sealing before they widen.

Selling the house

A finished garage stands out in a listing and tells buyers the home was cared for.

Inspection or safety

A shop, kitchen or warehouse needs a floor that's sealed, easy to clean and marked for safe traffic lanes.

How Epoxy Flooring Works in Irving

A typical two-car garage takes one to two days on site. Here's how the work goes from first call to a floor you can park on.

  1. Free on-site estimate

    We measure the floor, test it for moisture, map the cracks and joints, and walk you through flake colors and systems. You get a written quote.

  2. Diamond grinding

    Planetary grinders with dust-collecting vacuums open up the concrete and remove old sealers, paint and oil-soaked surface.

  3. Crack and joint repair

    Cracks are chased out and filled, pitted spots are patched, and control joints are handled so normal slab movement won't split the coating.

  4. Base coat and flakes

    A penetrating base coat goes down and vinyl flakes are broadcast into it while it's wet. Loose flakes are scraped and vacuumed once it sets.

  5. Clear topcoat

    A polyaspartic or urethane topcoat locks everything in and sets the final texture. We go over care instructions before we leave.

Frequently Asked Questions About Epoxy Flooring in Irving

How much does epoxy flooring cost in Irving?

The price depends on the size of the floor, how much crack repair the slab needs and which coating system you pick. We measure the floor, check it for moisture and cracks at a free on-site estimate, and give you a written quote before anything is scheduled.

Will the clay soil in Irving crack my new epoxy floor?

Expansive clay makes slabs move, so we treat every crack before coating. Hairline and settled cracks are ground out and filled with a repair compound, and active control joints are kept open or filled with a flexible material so normal slab movement does not split the finish.

Can you install an epoxy floor in the Texas summer heat?

Yes, but the schedule changes. Garage slabs and resin both get hot by midday, which shortens working time, so summer installs start early in the morning and we check the slab temperature before mixing anything.

Will hot tires lift my garage floor coating?

Hot tire pickup happens with thin, one-part epoxy paints from a hardware store. A diamond-ground slab with a high-solids base coat and a polyaspartic topcoat bonds deep enough that hot tires coming off a summer driveway do not peel it.

How soon can I park in my garage again?

With a polyaspartic flake system, most garages can take foot traffic the same evening and cars in about 24 to 48 hours. A traditional epoxy system usually needs several days before vehicles go back in. You get the exact timeline for your floor at the estimate.

How long does an epoxy garage floor last?

A professionally installed coating over a properly ground slab typically lasts 15 to 20 years in a home garage. The clear topcoat takes most of the wear and can be scuffed and recoated later instead of redoing the whole floor.

Do you have to grind the concrete first?

Yes. Diamond grinding opens the pores of the concrete so the resin locks in instead of sitting on top. Acid etching alone is weaker and is one of the main reasons DIY garage coatings peel within a year or two.

Is an epoxy floor slippery when wet?

A smooth high-gloss finish can be slick when wet. Flake floors have a natural texture, and we can add a fine anti-slip grit to the topcoat for garages that get rain tracked in, patios and commercial kitchens.
