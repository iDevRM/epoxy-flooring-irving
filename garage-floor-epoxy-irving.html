What Does Garage Floor Epoxy Involve?

A garage floor epoxy install is a full system, not a single coat. We diamond-grind the slab, repair cracks and pits, then lay a penetrating base coat and broadcast vinyl flakes into it until the surface is fully covered.

Once the base sets, loose flakes are scraped and vacuumed off and a clear polyaspartic topcoat seals everything in. Most two-car garages are done in one to two days, with cars back in about a day or two after the final coat.

When Do You Need Garage Floor Epoxy?

Most people call when the garage floor has started working against them. These are the most common signs:

  • Concrete dust gets tracked into the house
  • Oil and transmission fluid stains won't come out
  • A DIY coating is peeling where the tires sit
  • Hairline cracks are spreading across the slab
  • You're turning the garage into a gym, shop or hangout space

Why Does a Garage Floor Coating Peel?

Garage coatings almost always peel because of poor prep or the wrong product. Thin one-part epoxy paint never bonds into the concrete, so when tires heat it up in summer, it softens and lifts off with the tread. That's called hot tire pickup.

Moisture is the other cause. Water vapor pushing up through an unsealed slab can break the bond from below, especially after a wet North Texas spring. Grinding the surface and testing for moisture before coating is what prevents both problems.

What Affects the Cost of Garage Floor Epoxy in Irving?

Garage size is the starting point, but slab condition matters just as much. Older garages often have decades of oil soaked into the surface, patched spots and settled cracks from the clay underneath, all of which add prep time.

Newer homes can have a curing compound or sealer applied when the slab was poured, which has to be ground off completely. Your flake blend, stem-wall coating and any step or threshold work round out the quote. We'll price it after a free on-site look.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does garage floor epoxy last?

A professionally installed flake system over a ground slab usually lasts 15 to 20 years in a home garage. The topcoat can be lightly sanded and recoated down the road if it ever looks worn.

Can you coat a garage floor that has cracks?

Yes. Cracks are ground out and filled before coating. On clay-soil slabs we also treat control joints so normal seasonal movement doesn't split the new floor.

Will oil and fluids stain an epoxy garage floor?

No. A sealed epoxy floor doesn't absorb oil, antifreeze or brake fluid. Wipe spills up with a rag and a mild cleaner and the floor looks the same as before.

Do I need to empty the garage?

Yes, the floor has to be completely clear. Most homeowners move everything to the driveway or a spare room the night before, and we can help with heavy items like a fridge or tool chest.
